Overseas expansion on saturation

arrived 2026-07-31 — measured, user-ruled
When a civ's home landmass is full, it should look across the water instead of halting
Measured, not suspected. ring / inland-sea / oval report 0 overseas cities at 200t and at 400t with ring saturated (~117 cities against ~112 of capacity) — and continents, the shipped default map, behaves identically. Archipelago is the outlier at 5, which is the control proving the probe fires at all. Root cause is a trigger gap: the naval settlement route keys on a STRANDED settler, which a generous home arc never produces — the same shape as W8's diplomat intent, a working mechanism idle because the brain never asks. Acceptance is pinned to the probe's overseas-cities count rising above zero on ring AND continents with the control still firing and floors unmoved — explicitly not to a moved hash. Not a v1.0 blocker: the affected shapes are opt-in, the gap is opponent strength rather than correctness, and an unmeasured doctrine change does not belong in an RC. Spec: specs/unit-doctrine-v1x.md §8.
